Webinar on IoT and information technology brings together four speakers and student participants

By Administrator · Activities
20 September, 2026

The IEEE Industry Applications Society (IAS) Indonesia Chapter held the online Webinar IoT dan Teknologi Informasi (IoT and Information Technology Webinar) on Saturday, 13 December 2025, from 09:00 to 10:30 WIB via Zoom. Four lecturers from four Indonesian universities presented. Participants came from 12 institutions across several cities, and included students, lecturers and school teachers.

Highlights

The Chapter invited four speakers, each presenting a topic within their own field:

  • Prof. Dr. Ir. Fahmi, S.T., M.Sc., IPM (Universitas Sumatera Utara): Smart Garbage Electric Vehicle, Solusi transport sampah cerdas berbasis EV
  • Muhammad Yazid, B.Eng., M.Eng. (Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember): Algoritma Data Security untuk Perangkat IoT
  • Erwin Sutanto, S.T., M.Sc. (Universitas Airlangga): Residual Current Monitor Application with Fiber Optic
  • Dr. Purba Daru Kusuma (Telkom University): Collaborative Online Learning System

The session on data security for IoT devices was based on the paper "Simple authentication method for vehicle monitoring IoT device with verifiable data integrity", published in IEEE Internet of Things Journal in 2022. It looked at vehicle monitoring, where devices transmit data while moving and network quality changes constantly. The talk set out three security requirements: mutual authentication between device and server, integrity of the data stored in the server database, and anonymity of the device during transmission.

The presentation then explained why several common approaches are a poor fit for low-power hardware. Public key encryption is computationally expensive, and static shared keys allow a device to be cloned once the key is extracted. Pools of shadow identifiers use scarce device memory, and a trusted gateway becomes a single point of failure. As an alternative, the speaker described a protocol based on symmetric encryption and parallel hash chains. The talk covered the initialization phase, the handshake between device and server, and the handling of transmission failures. It closed with security and performance analyses of communication and computational cost.

Participation

The webinar drew a varied audience. Participants came from 12 institutions, mostly universities plus one vocational high school. Telkom University, Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS) and Universitas Negeri Malang were the largest groups. Other institutions represented were Universitas Muhammadiyah Ponorogo, Universitas Jember, Universitas Logistik dan Bisnis Internasional, Universitas NU Sidoarjo, Binus University, Universitas Brawijaya, Universitas Airlangga, UMKT and SMK Negeri 2 Pontianak.

Geographically, participants joined from several cities in Java, including Surabaya, Malang, Ponorogo, Jember, Sidoarjo and Bandung, and from Pontianak in West Kalimantan. Participants included students at undergraduate and graduate level, university lecturers and academic staff, and a vocational school teacher. Both state and private institutions took part, and the participants' fields ranged from engineering, science and computing to other disciplines such as nutrition.

The session ran on schedule and without disruption. A certificate of attendance was sent by email to participants after the webinar.

Next steps

The Chapter plans to continue organising activities on industry applications topics for students, lecturers and practitioners.
