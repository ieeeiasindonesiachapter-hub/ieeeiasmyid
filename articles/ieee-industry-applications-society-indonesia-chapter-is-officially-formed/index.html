IEEE Industry Applications Society Indonesia Chapter is officially formed

By Administrator · News
20 September, 2026

The IEEE Industry Applications Society (IAS) Indonesia Chapter has been formally established under the IEEE Indonesia Section. IEEE approved the Chapter with an effective formation date of 10 September 2025, giving engineers, researchers and industry practitioners in Indonesia a dedicated community for the industrial applications of electrical and electronic technologies.

Approval by IEEE

On 15 September 2025, Cecelia Jankowski, Managing Director of IEEE Member and Geographic Activities, confirmed that the requirements of the MGA Board Operations Manual had been met and that the Chapter had been formed. The letter was copied to IEEE Region 10 leadership, the IAS President and staff, and the IEEE Indonesia Section Chair.

Why the Chapter was formed

Indonesia needs to expand and modernize its industrial capacity to sustain economic growth, and much of its industrial output still depends on manual or semi-automated processes. Technologies such as the Internet of Things (IoT), artificial intelligence (AI) and advanced automation can improve efficiency and support real-time decision-making, but using them well requires collaboration, research and knowledge sharing. The Chapter aims to provide a platform where researchers, professionals and policymakers can connect, exchange ideas and support workforce development.

Founding members and officers

The Chapter began with a petition from IEEE IAS members of diverse backgrounds and institutional affiliations, spread across Indonesia from North Sumatra to West Java, East Java and Bali. Their institutions include Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember, Universitas Sumatera Utara, Universitas Airlangga, Institut Teknologi Bandung, Universitas Negeri Surabaya, Universitas Negeri Malang, Universitas Pendidikan Ganesha and Universitas Telkom.

The Chapter's officers for 2026 are:

  • Chapter Chair: Muhammad Yazid (Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember)
  • Chapter Vice Chair: Fahmi Fahmi (Universitas Sumatera Utara)
  • Chapter Secretary: Purba Daru Kusuma (Universitas Telkom) and Erwin Sutanto (Universitas Airlangga)
  • Chapter Treasurer: Pradini Puspitaningayu (Universitas Negeri Surabaya)
